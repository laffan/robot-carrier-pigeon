Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

RobotCarrierPigeon is a mail app for iPad and macOS that works with Gmail and Google Workspace accounts. This policy explains what data RobotCarrierPigeon accesses and what it does with that data.

The short version

RobotCarrierPigeon runs entirely on your device. It connects directly to Google and to no one else. There is no RobotCarrierPigeon server, no account with us, no analytics and no advertising. We never receive, store or see your mail, contacts or personal information.

Data RobotCarrierPigeon accesses

When you sign in with Google, you choose whether to grant RobotCarrierPigeon access to:

  • Gmail (https://mail.google.com/): your messages, threads, labels and drafts, so RobotCarrierPigeon can show, search, send, reply to, forward, archive, delete and label your mail.
  • Contacts (contacts.readonly and contacts.other.readonly): names and email addresses from your Google contacts and from people you've emailed, used only to suggest recipients while you write.
  • Basic profile (openid, email, profile): your name, email address and profile picture, used to identify the account inside the app.

How the data is used

RobotCarrierPigeon uses this data only to provide its mail features to you on your own device. It does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, does not transfer it to third parties, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored

  • Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's Keychain.
  • Settings, cached messages and draft text are kept in RobotCarrierPigeon's app storage on your device so the app loads quickly and works while you write.

None of this leaves your device except when RobotCarrierPigeon talks to Google's own services (Gmail, People and Google sign-in) on your behalf.

Removing your data

Signing out of an account in RobotCarrierPigeon revokes its access with Google and removes its sign-in token from your device. Deleting the app removes its cached mail and settings. You can also revoke RobotCarrierPigeon's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

RobotCarrierPigeon's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
