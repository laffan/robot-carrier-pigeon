Carrier

A minimal Gmail client for iPad and macOS. You write mail in Markdown.

Carrier covers the everyday parts of the Gmail app with less on screen. When you send a message, Carrier turns your Markdown into formatted HTML and includes the Markdown source as the plain-text version.

Signing in with Google

You add an account with Sign in with Google, using Google's own sign-in page. Carrier never sees your password. It asks Google for permission to:

  • Read, compose, send and organise your Gmail (https://mail.google.com/), so it can work as a full mail client.
  • Read your contacts and the people you've emailed (contacts.readonly, contacts.other.readonly), to suggest recipients as you type.
  • See your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile), to label each account in the app.

Carrier runs entirely on your device and talks only to Google. There is no Carrier server, and your mail is never sent anywhere else. See the privacy policy for details.
